Bet log & CLV

📓 Log every bet — the sim's TD props or your own player props (yards, receptions, anything) — and track CLV (did you beat the closing line?) and ROI. This is the only honest read on whether the sim actually makes money — aim for 50–100 bets before trusting it.
Same-game correlation: two scorers on the same team are positively correlated (when an offense scores a lot, several of its players cash together). Treat multiple "TAKE"s on one team as a same-game parlay leg set, not as independent edges. A model edge much larger than the market's usually means a missed injury, suspension or depth-chart change — verify before betting.
No college injury feed exists — ESPN publishes none and teams aren't required to. The model leans on who actually got the ball recently (a player who didn't touch it last game is scaled down; one with no touches all season, more so), but a mid-week injury or suspension won't show up until he misses a game. Check the team's availability report / beat writers and set OUT by hand before betting.
